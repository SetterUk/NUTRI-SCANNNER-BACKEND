Delete your HealthHeat account

From the app (fastest)

  1. Open HealthHeat and tap your profile picture.
  2. Open Profile and scroll to the bottom.
  3. Tap Delete account and data and confirm.

Without the app

Email [CONTACT EMAIL] from the Google account you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my HealthHeat account". We will delete it within 7 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

Your account, profile (including health conditions, allergies and medical notes), meal and weight logs, scan history and personalised results. Deletion from backups completes within 30 days.

What is kept

Product names, photos and ingredient lists you contributed to the shared product database remain, with no link to you.
