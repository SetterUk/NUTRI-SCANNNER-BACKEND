AI processing

To explain product ratings and answer NutriBot questions, the app sends the relevant product data and the parts of your profile and today's meals needed for the answer (for example goals, conditions, allergies and targets) to our server, which forwards them to Groq, our AI inference provider. Groq processes this to generate a reply. If you turn on the optional on-device model, NutriBot runs on your phone instead and those messages do not leave it.

Who we share data with

We only share data with service providers that run the app for us:

  • Google Firebase: sign-in, crash reporting, analytics and app configuration
  • Groq: AI text generation, as described above
  • Render (server hosting) and Neon (database hosting)
  • Open Food Facts: we look up the barcode you scan in this public database; no personal data is sent

We do not sell personal data or share it for advertising. We may disclose data if required by law.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access to your account data requires your sign-in token.

How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, meal and weight logs, scan history and personalised results are deleted from our server immediately, and from backups within 30 days. Product details you contributed stay in the shared product database without any link to you.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: Profile → Delete account and data. This deletes your account on our server and everything stored on your phone.
  • Without the app: follow the steps on our account deletion page.

Your choices

You can edit your profile at any time, leave optional fields (such as medical notes) empty, turn the evening reminder off in Profile, and deny camera, microphone or notification permissions in Android settings.

Children

HealthHeat is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
